This policy explains how your personal data is processed when you play the Rimfall mobile game (the “Game”).

In short: We never ask for your name, email, phone number or location. The Game creates a random player ID and a random nickname (like “GizliYunus”) for you; leaderboards, coins and cosmetics are tied to this anonymous account. The Game is free and supported by optional rewarded ads, occasional short ads and optional in-app purchases. Personalized ads require your separate consent; without it, ads are not personalized. We never sell your data. If you allow it, the Game sends anonymous gameplay statistics to help us improve it (you can turn this off at any time in Settings › Anonymous stats); it also sends anonymous crash reports so we can fix bugs. You can delete all of your data at any time with Settings › Delete my data.

This data is not linked to your name, email or any other identity information and is never sold. Ad partners process the advertising identifier, to the extent you allow it, under their own privacy policies. We do not derive your location from your IP address.

Data that stays on your device

  • Settings and local records: music, sound effect and vibration preferences, your best score and whether you have seen the tutorial are stored only on your device.
  • Share card: when you tap Share, the score image is created on your device and shared only with the app you choose. It is never sent to us.

Game Center (iPhone/iPad only, optional)

If you are signed in to Game Center on your device, your score and earned achievements are sent to Apple’s Game Center service under your Game Center account. Apple processes this data under the Apple Privacy Policy; your Apple ID and Game Center details are not shared with us. You can manage Game Center in iOS Settings › Game Center.

3. Service providers and international transfers

Server data is processed by our service provider Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services: Authentication, Cloud Code, Cloud Save, Leaderboards, Analytics, Diagnostics, LevelPlay, In-App Purchasing). Unity’s servers are located outside Türkiye, so this is an international transfer. See the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Advertising data is shared with the ad networks that serve the ads; the main network is Unity Ads (Unity Privacy Policy). Purchase receipts are sent to Apple for verification (Apple Privacy Policy).

Your nickname and score are shown to other players on the leaderboards. Data may be disclosed to competent authorities where required by law. Otherwise it is not shared with anyone.

4. Retention

  • Account, scores and game profile: until you delete them with Delete my data or ask us to delete them.
  • Anonymous statistics and crash reports: kept for Unity’s retention period (at most 2 years); turning off stats or using Delete my data automatically sends a deletion request.
  • The weekly league resets every week and the daily challenge leaderboard every day (at midnight Türkiye time).
  • On-device data is removed with Delete my data or when you uninstall the Game. The session token in the Keychain stays on the device after uninstalling so that the same account comes back when you reinstall; Delete my data removes it.

5. Security

All communication with Unity uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). The session token is stored in the iOS Keychain. Leaderboards cannot be written to directly; scores are saved only after server-side verification.

6. Deleting your data

In the Game, open Settings (gear icon, top right) › Delete my data and confirm. This deletes your leaderboard entries, coins, cosmetics, mission progress, player account and on-device records and sends a deletion request for anonymous statistics; the Game then starts over with a new anonymous account. Step-by-step guide: Deleting your data. You can also request deletion by email.

7. Your rights

You may ask us whether we process your data, request access to it, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, object to its processing, and — under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK), the EU/UK GDPR or other laws that apply to you — exercise any other rights you have. Email b2avargames@gmail.com; we respond free of charge within 30 days. Because the Game does not use names or emails, we may ask for your in-game nickname to find your account. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

8. Children

The Game does not ask for names, emails or contact details. Leaderboards show only random nicknames generated by the Game; players cannot enter their own names or message each other. The Game does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated. The current version is always published on this page; significant changes will also be announced in the Game.
